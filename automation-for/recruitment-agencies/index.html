Busy agencies tend to have one of two problems

Candidates go quiet once they're placed, so redeployment slips. A contractor you placed in March finishes on Friday. Nobody on your side had the end date in front of them. On Monday they start a new role, found by another agency that rang them first.

You re-source people already in your database. A new job comes in and the consultant searches from memory and LinkedIn. The ideal candidate is already on your books from two years ago. Nobody finds them until after the shortlist has gone.

Neither is about effort. Your consultants are busy doing the work that bills, and the follow-up is the first thing to slip.

If neither of those sounds like you, we'll say so on the call. There's no point building something for a problem you haven't got.

The follow-up happens whether or not anyone remembers

This is recruitment automation built around the tools you already use, your CRM, your inbox, your timesheet system, so the routine contact with candidates runs on dates and triggers rather than on someone's memory.

Messages go out in your consultants' names and sound like them. Anything that needs a judgement, a rate, an offer, a tricky reply, goes straight to a person. The system does the chasing. Your team does the talking.

Four recruitment automations agencies start with

Most agencies start with one.

1

First week check-in

The moment a placement starts, a candidate care check-in goes out from the consultant. Any reply that needs a person lands with them.

2

Contractor redeployment

Weeks before a contract ends, the consultant gets a nudge and the contractor gets a "what's next for you?" message, so you redeploy them before another agency rings.

3

Database job matching

When a new job is logged, your own candidate database is searched, past placements included, and a shortlist goes to the consultant. Old candidates get re-engaged instead of forgotten.

4

Timesheet chasing

Automated timesheet reminders go out on Friday and keep chasing until the timesheet is in. Only the stragglers reach a human.
