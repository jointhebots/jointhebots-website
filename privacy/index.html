Privacy Policy

The short version. We are a one-person automation business in Beverley. We hold business contact details for firms we think we can help, answers people give us on our own website, and records of our work for clients. We do not sell data to anyone. If you want us to stop contacting you, reply to any email and say so, and we will keep a note of that so it does not happen again.

2. What we collect, and why

If you visit this website

With your consent, we use Google Analytics to understand which pages people read and how they arrived. This tells us which of our articles are worth writing more of. It collects a device identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, and the pages you view. If you do not consent, it is not loaded and we learn nothing about your visit.

Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, including IP addresses, for security and to keep the site working. That happens whether or not you consent to analytics, because a website cannot be served without it.

If you answer the questions on one of our report pages

We store the four answers you give and the email address you enter. We use them to send you your result, to understand whether what we sell would suit your business, and to know which parts of the report people find useful.

If we send you a personalised report link

We would rather say this plainly than bury it. If we email you a link to a report with your firm's name in it, that link is unique to you. When it is opened, we record that it was opened and when. We use that to know whether the report reached a real person and whether sending it was worth doing. It does not follow you around the internet, and it tells us nothing about any other site you visit.

If you contact us or book a call

We keep what you send us: your name, email address, phone number if you give it, and what you wrote. Bookings are handled through Google Calendar, which records the time you chose and the details you enter.

If we contact your business by email

We find business contact details from public sources: company websites and their team pages, Companies House, professional body directories, and business listings. We use them to contact businesses about services we think are genuinely relevant to that business.

We rely on legitimate interests for this. In plain terms: we have a business reason to make contact, the message goes to a business address about that business, we keep it short, and we make it easy to stop. We have weighed that against the disruption of an unwanted email and think it is proportionate. You are entitled to disagree, and section 8 tells you how to make it stop.

If you become a client

We hold what we need to do the work and to run the business: contact details, the systems we connect for you, and records of what our automation sent on your behalf and when. That last part is the point of the product, and it is covered by our agreement with you.

4. Cookies

This site sets no cookies of its own. Cookies are set by three third parties:

  • Google Analytics, for the measurement described above. Loaded only with your consent.
  • Google Ads, where we are running advertising, to understand which adverts lead to enquiries. Loaded only with your consent.
  • YouTube, where a video is embedded in a page. These are set by YouTube when the video loads.

We also load fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address in order to serve them.

You can change or withdraw your cookie choice at any time using the cookie settings link in the footer of any page, or by clearing cookies in your browser.

8. How to make it stop

Reply to any email from us with "no thanks" or "stop" and we will add you to our suppression list and not contact you again. You do not need to explain, and you will not get a follow-up asking you to reconsider.

You can also email john@jointhebots.com and ask us to delete everything we hold about you.

9. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask what we hold about you, and get a copy
  • have anything inaccurate corrected
  • have your data deleted
  • object to us processing it, including for marketing, which we will always honour
  • ask us to restrict what we do with it
  • receive it in a portable format
  • withdraw consent at any time, where we relied on consent

Email john@jointhebots.com and we will respond within one month. There is no charge.

10. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, please tell us first and we will try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, or on 0303 123 1113.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal data, we will update this page and change the date at the top. If the change is significant and we hold your contact details, we will tell you.
